FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions parents and students often ask.

Are the lessons one-to-one?

Yes. Excel Tuition Centre focuses on private one-to-one online tuition rather than group classes.

Who can benefit from extra tuition?

Students may seek additional tuition to strengthen particular subjects, understand difficult topics, improve confidence, keep up with schoolwork or prepare for examinations.

Do you support international curricula?

Yes. We support students following international curricula and examination systems including Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, International Baccalaureate, OxfordAQA, AQA, OCR and WJEC/Eduqas.

Do you support IB students?

Yes. Support can be arranged for students following IB programmes including the Primary Years Programme, Middle Years Programme, Diploma Programme and Career-related Programme, subject to tutor availability.

Do you offer SAT and IELTS preparation?

Yes. We provide preparation and academic support for international tests including SAT, IELTS, TOEFL and ACT.

Can homeschooling students receive support?

Yes. One-to-one online tuition can supplement a homeschooling programme or provide focused support in particular subjects.

How do I enquire about tuition?

Complete the enquiry form below, email us, call us or contact us through WhatsApp. We will use the information provided to understand the student's needs and discuss the next step.
